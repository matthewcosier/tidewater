Holiday Tip

Hold M any time to open the Activity Wheel: pick a car, grab a jetski, fly off the carrier, launch the glider, or change the weather.

Press E to enter or leave any vehicle, board the ferry, or chat with locals. Press G on foot or in the water to recall your last ride right beside you.

Drive 9.4 km of coastal highways, mountain switchbacks, a rainforest gravel rally stage, and the 637 m suspension bridge across to Joey Island.

Grab a jetski from the beach hire stand or the Activity Wheel. Hold X on the water to ignite the rocket booster, and tap R after a wipeout to hop straight back on.

Ride the chairlift or hike to the 307 m volcano summit and launch the hang glider back down to the beach.

Launch the Kestrel jet from the offshore aircraft carrier, or board the Tidewater Spirit ferry and take her helm across the strait.

Feeling chaotic? Open World in the Activity Wheel to summon a tornado, tsunami, golden-hour sunset, or starry night.

Want a quiet afternoon? Press R near the water to cast a fishing line, sell your catch to Joe, and upgrade your boat with Marta.
